# Competitor Analysis (T-101) — lean snapshot

Date: 2026-10-14 · Author: Claude · Status: draft for owner review
Rule: **[F]** = fact with source · **[H]** = hypothesis / our interpretation.

## 1. Main competitors

| Game | Installs (Play) | Rating | Core mechanic | Monetization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brain Test: Tricky Puzzles (Unico Studio) | 100M+ [F1] | 4.7, ~9.8M ratings [F1] | Trick riddles: tap, drag, swipe, shake, "think outside the box" | Ads + IAP [F1] |
| Brain Out: Can you pass it? (Focus apps / EYEWIND) | 100M+ [F2] | 4.5, ~5.9M ratings [F2] | Trick riddles, humor, EQ/IQ "triple test", daily hints | Ads + IAP [F2] |
| DOP: Draw One Part (SayGames) | n/a (iOS checked) | 4.6, 233K ratings (iOS US) [F3] | Draw the missing part of a picture, 200+ parts | Ads; "No Ads" IAP $2.99; hints via rewarded ad [F3] |
| Draw Puzzle: Tricky Brain Test (Teos Oyun) | ~25M [F4] | 4.59, ~84K ratings [F4] | Drawing riddles | Ads (very frequent per reviews) [F4] |

All of them are **series**: Brain Test 1–5, Brain Out 2, etc. [F1][F2]. [H] Series exist because handcrafted levels run out → players finish, studio ships a sequel. **This is exactly the gap our "continuous content" vision targets.**

## 2. What players love [F1][F2][F5]
- "Aha!" / funny unexpected solutions; fun with family & friends.
- Variety of interactions (tap, drag, shake, tilt).
- Feels like it exercises the brain.
- Most common request: **more levels** [F5].

## 3. What players hate [F1][F2][F4][F5]
1. **Ads — the #1 complaint everywhere.** After nearly every level, 30s+ unskippable, closing an ad opens Play Store [F1]; "unbearable", ad every ~5 levels [F2]; "after every turn" [F4]. Players use airplane mode to dodge them [F5].
2. **Unfair / illogical answers**, or more than one valid answer not accepted [F1].
3. **Inconsistent interactions** — unclear whether to swipe, drag, tap or shake [F1].
4. Sloppy validation — in drawing games "a rough squiggle in the right area passes" [F4].
5. Bugs on specific levels (e.g., object won't fall) [F2]; can get stuck offline [F4].

## 4. Takeaways for us [H]
- Don't try to out-volume 100M-install incumbents. Win on **fairness + polish + fresh content**.
- An ad policy that respects players is a real differentiator *if* it still earns. Must be tested, not assumed.
- Puzzle fairness needs a process: every level playtested, alternate valid answers accepted, consistent interaction language with clear affordances.
- Continuous content (procedural families + daily challenge) answers the "more levels" request and removes the need for sequels.
